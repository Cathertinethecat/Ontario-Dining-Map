About the data

Restaurants. 69 restaurants: every $$$ and $$$$ restaurant found within about 10 miles of Ontario plus Pomona (OpenTable, TripAdvisor, Wanderlog and local press rosters, October 2026), plus mid-range ($$) sit-down restaurants in the same ring rated 4.5 or higher with 300+ reviews where a rating could be confirmed, and every award-recognized restaurant found across the wider region (Michelin Guide California, Wine Spectator Restaurant Awards; no James Beard recognition found in the region). Ratings and review counts are a snapshot quoted from the source named on each card: Google (as quoted by Wanderlog) where available, otherwise OpenTable, TripAdvisor or Restaurantji. Google and Yelp do not allow their ratings to be collected directly, so some ratings are from other sites and say so. Chains with more than about 20 locations are left out unless they are upscale steakhouse brands. Menu, Instagram, Facebook and reservation links and photos are taken from each restaurant's own site or its listings where found; blanks mean none was found, not that none exists. Positions placed by address, approximate.

Dining districts. Ten walkable restaurant clusters (historic downtowns, lifestyle centers, a food hall) outlined approximately around the cluster, with drive time from the Ontario Convention Center. Ontario's own downtown is the thinnest district in the ring relative to its hotel inventory. Boundaries and transit. City limits are the official boundaries from the Board of Equalization layer, loaded live; gray hatched areas are unincorporated county land. LA28 venues and rail are the same layers as the Ontario Hotel Map.
